A proprietary machine-learning model trained on millions of data points — matchups, pace, usage, efficiency, injuries, park factors, and line movement. It scores every pick 0–100, and that score becomes the letter grade. The AI writes the plain-English explanation; the model makes the call.

Is this a sportsbook?

No. PropGPT is an analysis tool. We never take bets, hold funds, or touch your bankroll. You bet wherever you already bet — PropGPT just makes sure you've seen the evidence first.

Which sports are covered?

Player props across NFL, NBA, MLB, and WNBA, plus game picks for NBA, MLB, college basketball, and marquee soccer — with seasonal specials like March Madness bracket tools.

No — and any app that says otherwise is lying to you. An A means the model's conviction is at its highest, and historically graded props hit 67.5% last season versus 45.5% for the average bettor. Sports stay sports. Bet what you can afford, every time.
